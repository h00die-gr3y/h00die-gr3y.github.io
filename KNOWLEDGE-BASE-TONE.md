# Knowledge Base tone baseline

The h00die-gr3y Knowledge Base should be personal without becoming gimmicky.

Preferred:
- first-person observations when they add practical context;
- direct descriptions of what was tested and why;
- concise troubleshooting notes;
- informal-but-professional wording.

Avoid:
- cutesy interjections such as "Oepsie" or "Oops";
- filler such as "nice!" or "cool PoC";
- migration framing such as "the original article" or "legacy page";
- overly academic wording where plain technical language is clearer.
