## Content boundaries

```text
Research
  specific vulnerability evidence, CVEs, exploit implementations

Articles
  long-form synthesis and methodology

Knowledge Base
  reusable procedures, concepts, troubleshooting and research workflows
```

- Web Applications

- Infrastructure
- IoT & Embedded
